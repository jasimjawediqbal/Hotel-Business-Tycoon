Title
“Echoes of a Forgotten Legacy”

(aka: The Last Will – Hotel Legacy)

Genre:
Story-driven 2D management game • Mystery • Choice-based narrative

Core Premise

You play as Arman Ayaan (name customizable) — a young man who escaped poverty by working low-end jobs in a distant city. After the death of his father and later revelations from his grandfather, he returns home to inherit a closed, decaying family hotel/motel on the edge of the city.

What starts as a simple survival and business challenge slowly unravels into a dark legacy involving hidden ledgers, buried deaths, corruption, rival corporations, and a question that defines the game:

Is legacy worth more than money?

CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER STORY + GAMEPLAY FLOW
Chapter 1: Homecoming to Ruins

Arman receives a late-night call.
His father has passed away, and his grandfather insists he return immediately.

Back home, Arman learns the truth:

The family never sold their old roadside hotel

It was shut down decades ago after “incidents”

The property is now in terrible condition — broken rooms, no reputation, almost no income

Key Characters Introduced

Maya – Arman’s mother (emotional anchor)

Rashid – Old hotel manager who stayed loyal

Grandfather – Knows more than he admits

Gameplay Introduced

Basic check-ins

Assigning rooms

Collecting payments

Paying bills

Repairing first rooms

⚠️ Income is low. Expenses never stop.

Chapter 2: The Locked Gates

Arman visits the hotel fully for the first time:

Vandalized rooms

Locals avoid the place

Rumors of “bad nights”

Grandfather admits:

“This place didn’t close because of money… it closed because of fear.”

Player Choice

Reopen slowly (room-by-room)

Delay reopening and earn money through city jobs

Consequences

Reopening unlocks long-term potential but financial pressure

Delaying weakens emotional bonds and future growth

Chapter 3: The Old Register (Hidden Ledger)

While cleaning reception, Arman finds:

A guest register from the 1970s

Names of activists, artists, smugglers, politicians

Pages torn out

Rashid becomes uneasy.
Grandfather avoids questions.

Player Choice

Investigate the past

Ignore it and focus on business

Consequences

Investigation unlocks mystery quests, hidden income, and danger

Ignoring keeps things safer but limits growth and endings

Chapter 4: Staff, Salaries & Rashid’s Debt

The hotel starts attracting guests.
More staff are needed:

Cleaners

Security

Parking staff

Rashid reveals the truth:
He owes money to a dangerous lender connected to local power figures.

Player Choices

Pay off the debt → Costly but earns loyalty

Negotiate → Risky

Expose the lender → Triggers retaliation

Consequences

Paid staff = high morale & better service

Unpaid staff = mistakes, bad reviews, sabotage

Failed choices lead to inspections, power cuts, threats

Chapter 5: The Rival’s Shadow

A corporate chain / local tycoon (Horizon Hospitality / Kamal Raza) approaches Arman.

“Sell the property. Let the past stay buried.”

After refusal:

Fake reviews

Supplier blockages

Break-ins

Power failures

Player Choice

Sell the hotel → Immediate money

Refuse and fight back → Hard mode

Consequences

Selling ends the legacy early

Refusing increases difficulty but unlocks true success paths

Chapter 6: The Ghost Room (Room 9 / Room 13)

Guests complain:

Sounds at night

Lights turning on

Someone crying

Grandfather finally confesses:
Someone died mysteriously in that room decades ago.

Player Choice

Uncover the truth

Seal the room permanently

Consequences

Truth turns hotel into a mystery / haunted destination

Sealing avoids fear but loses income and reputation potential

Chapter 7: The Lost Treasure & Ownership War

An old employee hints:

“Your father hid something… in case things went wrong.”

At the same time:

Legal documents surface

Ownership papers are incomplete

Someone is trying to claim the land

Player Choice

Search for the hidden treasure

Focus only on business

Fight legally

Settle quietly

Consequences

Treasure = financial rescue or crucial evidence

Legal victory secures full control

Settling weakens final endings

Chapter 8: Climax – Legacy or Escape

All systems collide:

Hotel upgrades

Staff morale

Reputation

Mystery resolution

Financial stability

Moral choices

The game asks one final question:

Did you save a building — or a legacy?

FINAL ENDINGS
🏆 1. Golden Legacy / Legacy Reborn (Best Ending)

History restored

Ghost room truth revealed

Rashid saved

Rival defeated legally

Treasure found

Staff loyal, reputation high

Result:
The hotel becomes a cultural landmark, known for mystery, history, and hospitality.
Arman fulfills his father’s dream and builds his own.

🌅 2. Hard-Earned / Quiet Success (Mid Ending)

Good management

Some mysteries ignored

Stable finances

Result:
The hotel survives and prospers — but never becomes legendary.
A peaceful life, lingering “what ifs”.

💰 3. Bittersweet Sale / Sold Silence (Bad Ending)

Hotel sold under pressure

Result:
Arman gains wealth but loses identity.
The hotel is demolished.
Legacy erased.

💀 4. Ruin / Lost Again (Worst Ending)

Debt overwhelms

Staff unpaid

Reputation collapses

Result:
Hotel shuts down permanently.
Arman returns to the same cycle he tried to escape.
